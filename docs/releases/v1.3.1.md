**Libell 1.3.1** — the first patch release on top of 1.3. It brings several vehicles in one app, a voice that tells you which wheel to raise, stepless wedge ramps, a fine-tune for the zero and a saved hitch height for caravans.

Open it at **https://moggleif.github.io/libell/** — if you already have it installed, it updates itself on the next start, or when you tap the name in the top-left corner.

## Highlights

- **Several vehicles.** Settings › Vehicle now lists your vehicles at the top: pick one, add a new one, rename or remove it. Each vehicle keeps its own measurements, ramps, zeros, targets, hitch height and sensor box; the phone's own calibration is shared. Switching vehicle restarts the app on the other one. A shared setup link can be added as a new vehicle instead of replacing the current one.
- **Spoken guidance.** Turn it on under Sound, and while the vehicle stands still the app says which wheel and how much — "Rear left, Step 2", "Crank up 20 mm", "Level" — so you can level without looking at the phone. A new plan is said once it has settled, an unchanged one only as an occasional reminder, and nothing while you drive. Only voices already on the phone are used, so it works offline; a phone without one says so, and the tones work as before.
- **Stepless wedge ramps.** A ramp without steps can now be picked as **Stepless wedge** with its maximum height. The wheel then says **Drive up** and exactly how far, never more than the wedge has. The Andersen Camper Leveler is in the list.
- **Fine-tune the zero.** Still not quite level after zeroing? Under the zero, **Left up**, **Right up**, **Front up** and **Back up** move it 0.1° at a time and show the total, with a button to reset. Retaking the zero starts it over.
- **Caravan: back to hitch height.** Save the drawbar's height while the caravan is still on the tow ball. Before you hitch up again, **Back to hitch height** shows only how far to crank the jockey wheel. If none is saved, the app quietly offers to save it first.

## Smaller things you may notice

- **More ramp models**, each with step heights taken from the maker's or seller's product page: Brunner Equalizer XL, Fiamma Jumbo, Hamron (Jula), Briv and TrutzHolm Auffahrkeil 5 t. **Fiamma Level Up Premium** is corrected to 70/130/170 mm.
- **EasyLevel**: the direction step starts with "long side forward" picked, since that is what the readings already assumed. Pick the other placement if your box sits that way.
- **Xparkle**: the box page's Debug info shows the box's serial number and firmware as raw bytes, and **Copy debug info** includes them.

## Under the hood

- Settings from 1.3.0 carry over: your current setup becomes your first vehicle.
- The deploy can now publish a patch version like this one (ADR 0007).
- Dependencies and CI actions are up to date.
- 2193 unit tests, up from 1995 at 1.3.0.

## Honest limitations

- Unchanged from 1.3.0: the EasyLevel support is built against the box's decompiled protocol and exercised with a simulator — **no physical EasyLevel box has ever been connected to this codebase.** The Xparkle box has been tested on one real box on Android.
- Spoken guidance, stepless wedges, the zero fine-tune and the hitch height are new in this release and have not yet been tried on a real vehicle.
- A sensor box needs Web Bluetooth: Chrome or Edge on Android or a computer. On iPhone it works through the Bluefy browser.
- The four non-Swedish dictionaries still have not been reviewed by a native speaker. Anything that reads wrong is a one-line fix — please report it under **Feedback**.
- The app deliberately refuses to run inside an iframe, so online "mobile viewer" sites show a blank page. That is the clickjacking guard, not a fault.
