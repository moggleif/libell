**Libell 1.4.0** — Libell now speaks 24 languages and can show lengths in inches. Settings for vehicles and targets are simpler, the main screen names the vehicle and target you are leveling toward, and a calibration or zero you did not mean to take can be undone with one tap.

Open it at **https://moggleif.github.io/libell/** — if you already have it installed, it updates itself on the next start, or when you tap the name in the top-left corner.

## Highlights

- **24 languages.** Besides Swedish, English, French, Spanish and German, the app is now in Dutch, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Danish, Norwegian Bokmål, Finnish, Czech, Hungarian, Romanian, Turkish, Croatian, Slovak, Slovenian, Estonian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Catalan and Icelandic. Pick one under Settings › General; the list is sorted by each language's own name.
- **Inches.** Settings › General › Show lengths in now offers mm, cm and inches. In inches every length reads in eighths ("1 ⅜ in"), fields take decimal inches, and spoken guidance says the length in words. Switching units never moves a stored length. A first run starts in inches where the phone's region uses them (US, Liberia, Myanmar) and in mm everywhere else.
- **Vehicle and target on the main screen.** Under the top bar, the vehicle you are leveling is named on the left (while you have more than one) and the active target on the right (as soon as one is saved). Tap either to open that part of Settings.
- **Undo a calibration or zero.** Calibrating the phone, zeroing the vehicle or a sensor box and the flip calibration now have one button and take effect at once. If the phone was tilted more than 15°, a warning says so and offers **Undo**, which brings back exactly what you had before.

## Smaller things you may notice

- **Names only when you need them.** Targets and vehicles no longer show a name field all the time. **Save current tilt as new target** and **New vehicle** ask for a name with a suggestion filled in; tap the chosen row again to rename it.
- **Vehicles are listed like targets**, with a ✕ on each row while there is more than one.
- **A new vehicle starts as a copy of the active one**: type, axle, measurements, ramps and drain side. Its zero, targets and sensor box start empty, since they belong to that vehicle.
- **Settings stays open** on Vehicle when adding, switching or removing a vehicle restarts the app.
- **A steep target is saved with the one Save button.** The warning only informs afterwards and says how to delete the target if it was a mistake.
- Swedish says **nolläge** for a vehicle's zero.

## Under the hood

- Every string now has a note on where it is shown and what it does, and the app's fixed terms (ramp, step, jockey wheel, …) have one locked word per language. Each translation was checked by translating it back into Swedish blind and comparing meaning; that pass also fixed 24 places in English, French, Spanish and German.
- Tests walk every shipped language instead of a fixed list of five.
- 2297 unit tests, up from 2193 at 1.3.1.

## Honest limitations

- Unchanged from 1.3.0: the EasyLevel support is built against the box's decompiled protocol and exercised with a simulator — **no physical EasyLevel box has ever been connected to this codebase.** The Xparkle box has been tested on one real box on Android.
- The 19 new languages are machine translations checked by back-translation, and none of the non-Swedish dictionaries has been reviewed by a native speaker yet. Anything that reads wrong is a one-line fix — please report it under **Feedback**.
- Inches and the Undo after a steep calibration are new in this release and have not yet been tried on a real vehicle.
- A sensor box needs Web Bluetooth: Chrome or Edge on Android or a computer. On iPhone it works through the Bluefy browser.
- The app deliberately refuses to run inside an iframe, so online "mobile viewer" sites show a blank page. That is the clickjacking guard, not a fault.
