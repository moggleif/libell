**LevelMate** is an installable web app (PWA) that helps you level your motorhome/RV. Lay your phone flat inside the vehicle — top edge toward the front — and the app shows which wheels to raise, by how much, and which ramp step to drive up onto.

This is the first release. Open it at **https://moggleif.github.io/levelmate/** and add it to your home screen.

## Highlights

- **Top-down RV diagram** as the heart of the screen, with a "Front ↑" arrow so you know how to place the phone. Wheels are **green** when they need no lift, **orange** when a ramp step fixes them, **red** when even your tallest step is not enough.
- **Per-wheel guidance on the diagram**: above each wheel the ramp step to drive up onto (e.g. "↑ 40 mm"), below it the missing height in whole cm.
- **Bubble level in the middle of the RV**, like a spirit level lying on the floor — centered and green when you are level, confirmed by a big "Your RV is level!".
- **Your vehicle, your ramps**: wheelbase, separate front/rear track widths, and the exact step heights of your leveling ramps in mm (semicolon-separated, e.g. `20; 40; 60`). The app recommends the step closest to each wheel's required lift.
- **Phone calibration**: put the phone on a surface you know is level and capture that as the zero point — cancels phone/case bias. Clearable at any time.
- **Dashboard warning lamps** in the top bar until you have saved your vehicle settings and calibrated — tap a lamp to jump straight to the right place.
- **Hamburger menu** with Settings, Calibration and Help; everything persisted in `localStorage` and validated on read, so corrupt or old data can never break startup.
- **Works fully offline** — everything is precached by the service worker, because a campsite often has no signal. Installs to the home screen on both Android and iOS.

## How it works

Tilt comes from the phone's gravity sensor (`DeviceMotionEvent`, with a `DeviceOrientationEvent` fallback), smoothed against jitter. From roll and pitch, each wheel's height is computed from your vehicle geometry; the highest wheel is the reference and every other wheel gets a lift figure — blocks only go _under_ wheels. The math is pure, unit-tested TypeScript (27 tests).

## Getting started

1. Open the app and (on iPhone) tap **Start** to grant motion access.
2. Open **☰ → Settings** and enter your wheelbase, track widths and ramp step heights.
3. **☰ → Calibration**: put the phone on a known-level surface and tap _Calibrate now_.
4. Lay the phone flat in the RV, top edge toward the front, and follow the diagram.
